The long end, six ways

Government bond yields, side by side. End-of-day figures published by each country’s treasury or central bank.

By country

Ranked by current yield, highest first

Yields are end-of-day and are not investment advice. Each figure is the closest official equivalent for that maturity, so they are comparable but not identical in construction — the US is a par yield, Germany and the UK are fitted zero-coupon spot curves, Canada uses its designated benchmark bonds, and Japan is the MoF rate. Spreads are quoted against the German Bund of the same maturity.

France is the exception: Banque de France stopped publishing OAT rates in July 2024 and no official daily replacement exists, so France shows Eurostat’s 10-year monthly average, drawn as a dashed line. It has no 30-year equivalent, so the 10-year stands in on the 30Y view and is labelled accordingly.
